Privacy Policy for Chessferatu

Effective date: March 8, 2026

Chessferatu is published by Half-Cocked Studios. This Privacy Policy explains what information this version of Chessferatu handles and how it is used.

1. Summary

Chessferatu is designed to run locally on your device.

This version of the app:

  • does not require an account
  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • does not use in-app advertising
  • does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • does not send your gameplay data to Half-Cocked Studios

2. Data stored on your device

To function correctly, Chessferatu stores gameplay and settings data locally on your device. This can include:

  • audio settings
  • engine depth and gameplay settings
  • opening progress, scores, streaks, and defeated bots
  • adaptive book-move history and diagnostics-related local state
  • temporary in-process game state used for resume behavior

This information is used only to operate app features and personalize your local gameplay experience on your device.

3. Data sharing

Half-Cocked Studios does not receive, sell, or share your local gameplay data from this version of Chessferatu.

4. Permissions and device access

Chessferatu does not use your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or advertising ID for gameplay.

The app uses standard device storage made available to the app sandbox in order to save settings and progress.

5. Third-party code

Chessferatu includes third-party software components such as the Arasan chess engine and other open-source libraries. These components are used to provide local gameplay and audio features. They do not change the statement above that this version of the app does not transmit your gameplay data to Half-Cocked Studios.

6. App stores and platform providers

If you download Chessferatu through Google Play, the Apple App Store, or another platform, that platform may collect information separately under its own terms and privacy policies. This Privacy Policy covers Chessferatu itself, not the store or device platform provider.

7. Children

Chessferatu is a game and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, use the support contact listed on the store page for Chessferatu.
