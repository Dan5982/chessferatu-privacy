Privacy Policy for Chessferatu

Effective date: March 15, 2026

Chessferatu is published by Half-Cocked Studios. This Privacy Policy explains what information the free version of Chessferatu handles and how it is used.

1. Summary

Chessferatu is designed to run locally on your device.

This version of the app:

  • does not require an account
  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • includes in-app advertising in the free build
  • uses third-party ad SDKs to request, deliver, cap, and measure ads
  • does not send your gameplay data to Half-Cocked Studios

2. Data stored on your device

To function correctly, Chessferatu stores gameplay and settings data locally on your device. This can include:

  • audio settings
  • language, engine depth, and gameplay settings
  • opening progress, streaks, defeated bots, and difficulty-based progression data
  • adaptive book-move history and diagnostics-related local state
  • persistent local game-state snapshots used to resume an in-progress game

This information is used only to operate app features and personalize your local gameplay experience on your device.

3. Data sharing

Half-Cocked Studios does not receive, sell, or share your local gameplay data from this version of Chessferatu.

The free build may communicate with ad providers such as Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads so that ads can load, display, and be measured. Those providers may process device and network identifiers under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Permissions and device access

Chessferatu does not use your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location for gameplay.

The app uses standard device storage made available to the app sandbox in order to save settings and progress.

The free build also uses network access, network-state access, and the Android advertising ID permission for ad delivery and related ad operations.

5. Third-party code

Chessferatu includes third-party software components such as the Arasan chess engine, Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads components, and other open-source libraries. These components are used to provide gameplay, audio, and ad-supported free-app features. They do not change the statement above that Half-Cocked Studios does not receive your local gameplay data from the app itself.

Unity's published privacy and legal materials describe ad-related data categories that may include device identifiers, the advertising ID, IP-based network information, play-session and monetization events, ad interaction data, and certain ad-performance data. Some of those categories may also be used by Unity or its ad partners for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, measurement, and, where allowed, personalized advertising.

6. App stores and platform providers

If you download Chessferatu through Google Play, the Apple App Store, or another platform, that platform may collect information separately under its own terms and privacy policies. This Privacy Policy covers Chessferatu itself, not the store or device platform provider.

7. Children

Chessferatu is a game and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, use the support contact listed on the store page for Chessferatu.

Unity also publishes privacy information for its advertising services at: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy and docs.unity.com/en-us/grow/levelplay/platform/legal-resources/apple-privacy-questionnaire.
